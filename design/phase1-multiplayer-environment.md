# Phase 1 — Approved Multiplayer Sanctuary Environment

Status: **APPROVED for Phase 1 implementation (2026-10-23)**. This document supersedes conflicting earlier world/base layout plans in master.md for the new redesign. Earlier V2/V3 expedition documents remain historical references, not instructions to rebuild their layouts. No gameplay systems approved for this phase.

## Game direction (future phases, not Phase 1)
Creature Collection × Tycoon × Mountain Progression × Competitive Raiding. One mountain with harder elevations and guardians; egg extraction and wild encounters; three-creature teams with auto-attacks and manual specials; guardian-gated progression; personal habitats, passive income, training, XP, abilities, evolution; offline competitive raids, live friend challenges, trophies, achievements and co-op exploration.

## Locked Phase 1 environment
- Brand-new Roblox Studio place, **8-player server target**. Preserve original ClimbAndCollect.rbxl, backups and prior project work. Never publish without explicit permission.
- **Eight 110 × 120-stud plots in one horizontal row**, all facing toward the mountain. **8-stud landscaped buffers** between plots; total row frontage **936 studs** (8×110 + 7×8). Keep footprints level and usable.
- Each plot: **open courtyard** for future construction and **one 35 × 30-stud Enchanted Alpine starter lodge at the rear**. Buildings are decorative/environmental only at this stage.
- **Three gates centered together across from the middle plots**, one grand **~60-stud-wide** center gateway and two **~45-stud-wide** side gates. Gate purposes TBD; build real accessible structures, do not assign gameplay functions.
- **Only essential short landscaped access space** between plots and gates. **No shared promenade, plaza, Expedition Guild, Hall of Champions, training hub, or other activity facility.** Direct, quick mountain access is a priority.
- Architecture: **Modern Alpine × Enchanted Fantasy**. Premium timber, stone, glass, decorative rooflines, tasteful glowing magical accents.
- Plot/estate boundaries: **hybrid natural** — stone and timber details, hedges, trees, landscaped slopes, rock formations. Clear boundaries without a prison-wall feel.
- Terrain: **sheltered mountain basin with prominent elevated terraces**, retaining walls, cliffs, pines, waterfall accents. Gateway terrace roughly **12–18 studs** above access level, with wide steps and accessible ramps. The mountain beyond gates needs an impressive visual backdrop only; full mountain traversal/progression is deferred.
- Lighting: **Bright Alpine Daylight** — clear sky, vibrant vegetation, crisp readable light and soft shadows.
- Landscaping: intentional, handcrafted flowerbeds, boulders, trees, lanterns, small magical accents. Keep open courtyard and paths readable, optimize for mobile.
- All sizes other than locked plot/lodge footprints are planning targets, to validate in Studio before final placement.

## Phase 1 scope and gate
Build the new environment only: eight plots, eight lodges, three monumental gates, short access paths, boundaries, terraced terrain, landscaping, lighting and decorative detailing. **Do not implement** creatures, habitats, tycoon systems, guardians, raids, combat, progression, trophies, leaderboards, or additional social buildings.

Work in controlled steps. First inspect repository, master.md, CLAUDE.md, and existing Studio project for reusable art/workflows; create a separate new place and checkpoint without modifying the original. Establish the full-server spatial blockout and test scale/sightlines/movement before detailed construction. Produce aerial and ground-level screenshots, save checkpoints and the separate place file, document verified performance (including mobile testing when possible), then **STOP after Phase 1 for user visual approval**. Do not proceed automatically.
