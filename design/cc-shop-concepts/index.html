PURCHASE HANDOFF

Keep the next step open.

Every offer opens the same purchase path, including HUD chips and map displays. Click any price to explore the demo. Choose the insufficient-Robux scenario to see the handoff.

  1. Choose an offerItem, exact contents, total benefit, current Robux price.
  2. Roblox checkoutNative purchase prompt; Roblox handles available Buy Robux options.
  3. Confirm receiptGrant once on the server, save, then update every surface.

This browser demo simulates the handoff. It cannot read a Roblox balance or purchase anything. Roblox controls top-up packages, platform/account restrictions and the checkout UI; an exact-shortfall purchase cannot be guaranteed. Preserve the selected offer on cancel or return. Verify in a published test experience before release.
