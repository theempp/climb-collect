**P1 Plot + promenade (`HomeLayout` v2, `HomePromenade.refit`).** The v1 bake is refitted in place: plots moved and resized, lodges scaled, plot-front walls shortened to the 100 frontage, lanes/kerbs/channel/water shortened to the moved south pool, lanterns re-anchored, divider hedges moved and trimmed to 130 depth (224 pieces removed), gate wing walls shortened to the new basin. Berms regenerated by the existing `HomePromenade.terrain()` with the v2 span. No new art was invented; everything is existing art moved, trimmed or scaled.

**P2 Showcase + decor (`HomeDecor.luau`, `Home` action).** Versioned subrecord `profile.tycoon.home = {version=1, owned, decor, showcase}`, fail-closed in `Tycoon.valid` (old profiles without it stay valid). Server-validated buy/place/clear/showcase; only on your own plot. Decor props and pedestals are built from existing production colours plus the owner's kingdom accent. Kingdom skins = the existing palette (`cosmetic`).

**P3 Production feel (visual only).** Conveyor eggs ride the whole spine toward the bank; stock eggs pile up beside each gauge; a beacon pulses when a store is full; machine wheels spin once installed; collect and bank fire a ring/coin burst (`TycoonFeel.client.luau`). Creatures in habitats and on displays idle-bob. Reduced motion / simplified VFX keep a static state; everything is distance culled. Machine labels show only within 22 studs so they don't stack over habitat labels.

**P4 Visiting (`Inspect` action).** Any player in the sanctuary can tap a habitat or display. The server builds a minimal view (species, level, rate, kingdom, decor ids, trophy count) — no coins, eggs, bag, ids or upgrade levels. Visitors cannot collect, bank, assign, upgrade or decorate (all owner-gated by `atHome`). 0.5 s per-player cooldown on taps.

**P5 Economy.** Production rates, upgrade curves, offline cap and bonus-egg rhythm are **unchanged**: the smaller plot doesn't change any economic input, and the last pacing simulations kept them. New: decor is an optional coin sink, provisional costs 0–120 (450 total for all, below the first rank of every upgrade path ×2). PROPOSED, not balanced by human play.

**P6 Creature presentation.** Habitat and display creatures idle in place. The finished Mossbun egg/break/hatch at the incubator is unchanged from Review06. Other species remain procedural (no new art exists).
