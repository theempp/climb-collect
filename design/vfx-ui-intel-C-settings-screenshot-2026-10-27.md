## What it adds on top of the HUD v2 kit
1. A **glass/neon "magical" skin** (violet + hot pink) instead of the blue + yellow candy skin. This is a palette conflict with the approved HUD v2 (see below).
2. **Sparkle flares and halftone** as ambient decoration on panels.
3. A repeatable **row pattern** (icon tile, title, one-line caption, control on the right) that makes dense hubs feel organised.
4. A **player level card** in the settings footer, a free retention hook.
5. Settings that fit this game: **Camera Shake toggle and Damage Numbers toggle**. Camera shake is central to the planned intensity pass and must have an off switch (accessibility). The current Settings hub has only Accessibility, Audio and Quick tabs.
