HUD REVISION · GLASSBLOOM + GOLD

Less HUD, same game

The same Glassbloom look with the slamming RUN! and the reward strip, rebuilt around Roblox's own menu and chat buttons. During a chase the HUD now covers less than half the screen area it used before. Menus are laid out with real grid and scroll areas, and a check on this page proves nothing hangs out of a panel at three phone sizes. Concept only: nothing is built in Studio.

What changed

  • Roblox's menu and chat buttons are drawn in every frame as grey placeholders. Our top row sits right of them, inside the 58 px topbar row, and nothing else goes above 66 px on the left.
  • Level and XP left the permanent HUD. The ring around the Creatures button shows XP and level; tapping it opens the player card.
  • The "Carry Mossbun home" widget is gone. A 2.5 s pop-up announces the carry and shrinks into a 52 px portrait with a distance ring. The safe gate gets a world beacon, plus an edge arrow when it is off screen.
  • RUN! is now the 1.5 s guardian alert; afterwards only the pink edge glow stays. The dock fades to 25 % and shrinks to three buttons until you tap it or reach safety.
  • Coin toasts moved from the bottom-left (the thumbstick) to just under the coin pill.
  • Collection, Shop and Settings use grid and flex with scroll areas: fixed side tabs, a visible scrollbar, a fade at the bottom edge, section headers, and a Starter Pack card that stays pinned in the shop.
  • The shop is redesigned (see "The shop" below): gold-trimmed panel, a holographic Starter Pack, and a different shape for each kind of offer.
  • The shop tab "Bats" is now "Skins", looks only. Bat level changes theft knockback, and paid power in theft is ruled out.

Containment check

Runs in this page at each phone size. HUD frames: every box on screen, none touching Roblox's buttons, and no permanent box inside the thumbstick zone, the keep-clear centre, or another box. Menus: every element inside its panel, or inside its scroll area's width; no scroll area that scrolls sideways; no clipped label. The two old frames are controls and should fail.

Notes for the Studio build later

  • The top row already rides in the topbar row in UIPresentation.hudLayout when GuiService.TopbarInset is passed in. The menu and quick tiles that sit beside Roblox's buttons there go away in this layout; only the coin pill and gear stay, flush right.
  • Scroll areas map to a ScrollingFrame with AutomaticCanvasSize Y, ScrollBarThickness 6 in the pink token, UIGridLayout or UIListLayout plus UIPadding. The bottom fade is a UIGradient frame hidden once CanvasPosition reaches the end.
  • Roblox has no sticky position, so the Starter Pack card lives above the ScrollingFrame and shrinks when CanvasPosition.Y passes 8.
  • The containment rule on this page can become the pass condition for mountain/tools/hud_overflow_audit.client.luau: each descendant inside its panel, or inside its ScrollingFrame's width.
