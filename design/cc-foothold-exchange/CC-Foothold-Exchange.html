Prism’s bright storefront, Expedition’s featured offers, Foothold’s physical displays and Crown’s permanent progression, brought into one mountain market.

Draft economy

Permanent earned-coin tiers: 2× → 4× → 6× → 8× → 10× → 12×. Prices: 3 / 9 / 19 / 39 / 79 / 149 Robux. Only the next tier is available. Purchased coin packs never multiply; Summit Points stay earned.

Camp Glow saves 50% versus the two separate items at 19 Robux each. “Best value” compares coins per Robux across the four packs. The timely-collection bonus remains an unresolved proposal and is not changed here.

Purchase-flow laboratory

All checkout screens are simulations. Choose enough or insufficient Robux inside an offer, cancel, or simulate a return from top-up. No real balance, payment or Roblox connection is used.

Eventually: native MarketplaceService prompts, server-validated receipts and durable ownership. Roblox controls top-up packages and account/device restrictions; an exact-shortfall top-up is never promised.
