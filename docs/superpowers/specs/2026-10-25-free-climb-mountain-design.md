## Intent and selected approach

Make the mountain immediately understandable: **climb toward a visible egg, tap to break it, reveal a creature, escape downhill, cross into sanctuary, carry it home.** Keep the excitement of a guarded collection run and the identity of one magical mountain. The owner wants the straightforward concept of Steal An Egg and Break and Steal an Egg with thoughtful improvements, rather than extra progression systems.

The owner chose an open mountain face over a single winding trail or three broad terraces. All six existing sections stay, with simpler layouts and a distinct danger in each. Every section is attemptable from the start. Tougher guardians and hazards higher up provide the practical barrier; guardian wins, collection milestones and numerical access requirements do not gate climbing.

The gain over the winding-trail approach is a visible target and direct downhill return. The tradeoff is that sightlines, downhill geometry and guardian pursuit must carry more of the challenge. Separate sections remain recognizable rather than becoming one uniform egg field. Six-section retention was explicitly selected in this continuation; it was previously only current-source evidence.

## Confirmed owner decisions

- Break and reveal on the mountain using a visible player pickaxe. Tap once per strike; shell cracks and chips communicate progress. Strike count and cadence are tuning, not approved numbers.
- Sculpted-fantasy creatures with defined anatomy, expressive faces and springy animal motion. Small creatures use a two-arm cradle.
- First pickaxe strike alerts the guardian and begins its approach while the player is breaking the egg.
- Keep increasingly difficult guardian fights AND increasingly dangerous pursuits, alongside a few mountain hazards.
- Optional normal guardian victory gives a brief raid opening plus companion XP. The guardian resumes defense afterward. Harder Trials earn the guardian collectible.
- A guardian catch or serious drop-causing fall loses that unsecured creature; retry another egg. No rescue pack/window. Previously secured creatures remain safe.
- Crossing the mountain exit into sanctuary permanently secures the creature. Carry it home afterward for placement. Mountain checkpoints do not secure it.
- Retain Blossom Gardens, Azure Falls, Sunstone Desert, Violet Crystals, Inferno Heights and Astral Crown, each with simpler layout and distinct danger.

No trip duration, tool shop, paid access, new currency or player theft was selected. Existing collection, companion and tycoon work remains in scope; this redesign does not cancel unrelated launch requirements.

## A complete run — proposed connective rules

At the mountain entrance the player can see nearby eggs, the next section landmark and the sanctuary exit. Climb freely to a target. No mandatory puzzle, mover circuit or boss victory interrupts the main egg run; existing work remains preserved for deliberate reuse where it helps.

On the first accepted strike, the shell reacts and the relevant guardian visibly turns/approaches. A readable warning identifies that guardian and its direction. Approach distance, strike work and escape distance must leave a fair response opportunity. Do not lock the player into an unskippable reveal cinematic while danger advances. Reveal feedback and pickup remain quick and readable, with reduced-motion support.

**Proposal: one physically carried unsecured creature per player.** Breaking a new egg while already carrying is unavailable with a clear explanation. Keep the first revealed creature reserved to its breaker; other players cannot take it. After reveal, use one obvious pickup action; the creature stays near its opened shell until picked up or the attempt ends. Do not permit revealing an unlimited pile to select only the best reward: one active egg/reveal reservation per player, with a builder-tuned expiry. Abandonment releases the encounter, not a permanently owned reward.

Small creatures use the chosen cradle. **Proposal:** larger species and guardian rewards use a species-appropriate portable collectible form, distinct from the huge defending boss. Review their silhouettes and carry poses at avatar scale; do not force birds, fish and heavy species into the same anatomy or shrink an entire boss arbitrarily. The exact art solution remains an asset review task. Equipped companions remain separate from the carried reward.

The player returns down clearly readable routes. A direct escape means readable downhill travel with intended landings, not a safe leap from the summit or an automatic teleport. Short jumps and ordinary landings retain cargo. Clear dangerous-drop cues distinguish a serious fall that loses it; exact detection is builder tuning, verified on actual terrain.

**Proposal:** defeat/serious-fall respawn at the latest reached section rest point, empty-handed. Rest points help retry but never bank creatures. Manual reset also forfeits unsecured cargo. No loss of secured roster, coins or home production as an extra penalty. Failure feedback names the lost unsecured creature and points to retry.

At the exit the guardian stops before sanctuary. The creature receives one permanent ownership record and a short 'Safe!' acknowledgment. **Proposal:** it cannot fight, earn production income or be traded/equipped before this event. Once secured it can be assigned through existing roster systems; home placement is the physical celebration and habitat setup, without a second ownership gate. If the player leaves before placing it, it remains in the roster and can be placed later.

Carrying across a lower section alone does not automatically summon that section's guardian. A new strike or deliberate combat encounter can alert it. This keeps the chosen upper-section danger meaningful while avoiding an unreadable six-guardian pileup. Mountain hazards still matter on descent.

## Optional fights, Trials and multiplayer — proposed details

Normal combat is a deliberate engagement using the companion battle system. A chase does not force a battle menu. Avoid splitting attention between manual pickaxe strikes and companion ability inputs: normal fights begin empty-handed, with no pending revealed creature. A player can instead attempt the egg under pressure without fighting.

**Proposal:** the visible shared guardian retreats/rests after a normal win, granting a short public opening in that section. Nearby players can use it; XP goes to eligible battle contributors, without a last-hit requirement. This creates understandable cooperation. Respite never disables hazards, never becomes permanent and cannot be extended indefinitely through overlapping fights. One section guardian cannot simultaneously chase and appear in a contradictory separate arena battle; its state and return warning must be visible. Public timing and reward eligibility require multiplayer tests.

**Shared-state proposal, clarified after builder review:** normal combat happens against the mobile section guardian; it cannot pin the guardian in an arena while others break eggs freely. Several players may raid together. First strikes add those players to its active threat set and turn its approach toward a breaking egg; ties use proximity, with stable targeting between new alerts. Fighting players can damage the approaching guardian using companions, but cannot lock its attention away from every egg. When no egg is actively being broken, it pursues an eligible raider/fighter within its bounded territory. Uninvolved players never enter its threat set solely through proximity. Losing or leaving removes that player's threat; all eligible threats gone returns it to defense. Exact target dwell/attack scheduling is tuning. This makes shared distraction possible without a combat queue or an indefinitely captive guardian. The owner is reviewing this behavior as a proposal, not an already selected multiplayer rule.

The harder Trial remains optional guardian collection after a normal win, preserving combat mastery rather than a section-access requirement. **Proposal:** Trials use a clearly marked magical challenge manifestation in the optional combat space, separate from the living mountain defender; winning a Trial does not suppress the defender. A successful eligible Trial grants one reserved portable guardian reward to carry through the same exit safety rule. Losing that unsecured reward allows another eligible Trial attempt; mark permanent capture only when secured. Existing secured guardian ownership is preserved and repeat Trials do not duplicate one-time ownership rewards. Exact mastery checks, XP quantities and repeat rewards stay with the builder using current combat rules as evidence, not assumed unchanged approval.

No player-base theft or ability to knock another player's creature away is added. Preserve server-authoritative egg reservation, custody, combat contribution and ownership. Competition for unclaimed eggs remains understandable; no new party system is required merely to perform the basic run. Shared pursuit must not retarget onto unrelated players solely to grief them.

## Travel, disconnect and existing saves — proposed rules

- **Unsecured:** no mountain shortcut/extraction teleport may secure or transport the creature past the escape. If using travel requires abandoning the run, show a clear warning and allow cancellation. Walking/climbing remains available. Reaching a mountain rest point does not secure anything.
- **Disconnect/leave before the exit:** forfeit the unsecured attempt, as with reset; reconnect empty-handed. Make the rule visible before committing. Durable server-recorded exit ownership survives a disconnect after crossing, even before the arrival animation completes. Accidental disconnections have the same gameplay consequence; no unapproved recovery timer is implied.
- **After the exit:** sanctuary/home travel may carry the presentation or resolve it into the secured roster. No second risk or second reward. Home placement can resume later.
- **Existing stored eggs:** preserve their identities, species and existing incubation/hatching path as a legacy option. New mountain acquisition uses break/reveal/carry and needs no home incubation. Keep old facilities and assets preserved pending a separate visual reuse decision.
- **Existing owned creatures:** preserve roster, levels, guardian captures, habitats, production and currencies. Production eggs remain non-hatchable tycoon currency, clearly distinguished from mountain discovery eggs.
- **Migration:** no existing unsecured cargo is silently destroyed when switching versions. Map existing carried eggs into a recoverable migration entitlement, outside the repeatable new-run rule, with preserved IDs/species. Select and verify an exact migration policy before rollout; this is a release prerequisite, not reason to delete old profiles. No rollout/publication is authorized here.

## Engagement improvements and what stays simple

The main improvements proposed are readable egg/guardian/exit sightlines; tactile cracks and expressive reveals; an animal visibly protected in the player's arms; danger learned through distinct tells; clear safe-arrival feedback; and optional combat that helps a raid while rewarding companion investment. Collection variety and existing home production give successful returns a purpose. None of these is a measured retention claim.

Do not add treadmill grinding, speed gates, obligatory shops, more currencies, forced multi-stage puzzles or long cinematics to manufacture progression. Higher sections should challenge learned movement and stronger companions without making every early attempt mathematically impossible. Time ascent, breaking, pursuit and return separately in human tests before setting trip-duration targets. Existing older expedition timing is not a new approved goal.
